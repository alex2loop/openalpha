# Gold analysis

Use for any gold instrument. Two different ones share the word in this product, so say which you are analyzing before anything else.

## Global gold

The durable drivers, in order of usual importance:

- **Real yields.** Gold pays no coupon, so its opportunity cost is the real yield. Falling real yields are the single most reliable tailwind.
- **Dollar strength.** Priced in dollars; a stronger dollar is a mechanical headwind.
- **Central bank demand.** Slow-moving, well-documented, and the reason the real-yield relationship has loosened in some periods. Cite the reported flows, not the theory.
- **Crisis demand.** Real but short-lived, and it reverses. Do not build a multi-month thesis on it.

Jewellery and industrial demand are seasonal and rarely decide a move.

## Domestic VN gold

Domestic gold is the global price *plus a premium* set by import quota, local supply, and policy. Analyze the two separately:

1. The global leg, as above.
2. The premium itself, which has its own history and its own mean. A wide premium is a policy bet; it can compress even when global gold rises, leaving a domestic holder flat or down.

Quoted domestic prices carry a dealer buy/sell spread that is wide by the standards of any listed market. The spread is a real, immediate cost on entry and exit, and a domestic gold position must clear it before it makes money. State it.

Different physical forms - bar and ring - trade at different premiums and are not interchangeable. Do not compare a ring quote to a bar quote.

## Output

Name the leg driving the case, the current premium relative to its own history for the domestic instrument, and the spread cost the position must clear.
