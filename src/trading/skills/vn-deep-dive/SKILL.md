# VN deep dive

Use when a name has cleared the daily routine and deserves real work, or when the operator asks to understand a holding properly.

## Structure

1. **What the business actually does.** Revenue by segment and by customer. If one customer or one project carries the business, that is the analysis, not a footnote. Before going further, state the business and its two drivers in one paragraph from the filing; if that paragraph cannot be written, the name is outside competence today, and the deep dive stops here and says so.
2. **How it makes money.** Unit economics where they exist; margin structure and its direction with the reason for the direction.
3. **Ownership and governance.** Controlling shareholder, state ownership, insider holdings, and whether insider shares are pledged. Pledged shares turn a price fall into forced selling. `capital-allocation` reads what management did with five years of cash.
4. **The financials, restated.** Start from the ANNUAL ACCOUNTS evidence (in chat, `market_accounts`), including its newest quarters; restate from the filing only what it cannot show. Strip one-offs. Reconcile profit to operating cash flow across several years. Check receivables, inventory, and the maturity wall.
5. **The sector context.** Where the industry is in its cycle, and what policy or input cost sets its economics. `moat-analysis` names why the returns persist, or that they will not.
6. **Valuation.** As per `vn-valuation`, and `intrinsic-value` where a long hold is the question.
7. **The specific way this loses money.** One paragraph naming the mechanism - not "market risk". This is the part that makes the rest useful.

## Sources, in order of authority

Audited annual report, then quarterly filings, then exchange disclosures and corporate action announcements, then transcripts. Sell-side notes are a source of questions, not of conclusions: domestic coverage skews structurally positive and rarely publishes a sell.

## Discipline

- Cite the filing and the period for every number that carries weight.
- Prefer a checkable number to a confident adjective.
- Say plainly which parts you could not verify. An unverified load-bearing claim is what the claim verifier exists to catch.

## Output

A thesis in three sentences, the evidence table behind it, the invalidation condition, and the unverified residue.
