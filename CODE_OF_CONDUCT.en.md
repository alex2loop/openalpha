# Code of Conduct

[🇻🇳 Tiếng Việt](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) &bull; 🇬🇧 English

## The short version

Be decent to people. Argue with the code, not with the person who wrote it.

This project is a trading research tool, so a lot of its discussions are arguments about
whether a number is right. Those arguments are the point. What is out of scope is turning
one into an argument about a person.

## Expected behaviour

- **Say what you measured.** "This is wrong" is a claim; "this is wrong, here is the run
  that shows it" is a contribution.
- **Accept a decline.** [CONTRIBUTING.en.md](CONTRIBUTING.en.md) lists the settled decisions and
  the reasoning, so a decline is usually a scope call rather than a verdict on your work.
- **Assume less context, not bad faith.** Most disagreements here are about a market's
  behaviour that one of you has seen and the other has not.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks — in issues, pull requests, commit messages, or
  any other project space.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind, including sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption: reopening a settled decision without new evidence, or pushing a
  change after it has been declined with a reason.

## Telling someone what to trade

OpenAlpha suggests and never executes without its operator. Telling another person in this
project what to buy, or presenting the software's output as advice to follow, is out of
scope in every project space. Discuss whether the code is correct, not whether a trade is
a good idea.

## Scope

Every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, release notes —
and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report a conduct problem through the private channel in
[SECURITY.en.md](SECURITY.en.md#reporting-a-vulnerability). It is the only private channel this
project has, and that page states where it goes and how long an acknowledgement takes.

Please do not open a public issue about a person's conduct.

A report is read by the maintainer alone, and what you send is not shared further without
your agreement.

## Consequences

The maintainer may edit or remove any contribution, and may block an account. Nothing here
requires a warning first: one serious incident is enough.
